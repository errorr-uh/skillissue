Discord Quest Script

Copy the script in one click or download it as a text file. Choose the guide that matches your device.

Notice: automating quests may go against Discord’s Terms of Service and could put your account at risk. Use it at your own risk.

PC · Discord desktop app

  1. Open the Discord desktop app and accept a quest.
  2. Open Developer Tools (Ctrl + Shift + I) and go to the Console tab.
  3. Paste the script and press Enter.

Mobile · Browser extension

  1. Download the extension ZIP.
  2. Install it in a mobile browser that supports extensions.
  3. Open Discord in that browser and start your quest.
